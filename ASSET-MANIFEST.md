## Truth rules
A product card or generated cutout is not confirmation of physical stock. Four concepts have specific generated cutouts; other references do not get unrelated or generic overlay images. External brand images remain inspiration/reference assets, not confirmed BLACKED products or collaborations. Confirm image permissions before commercial public reuse.
