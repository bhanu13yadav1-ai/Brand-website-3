# Release notes — v1.0.0

## Added: BLACKED Intimate Wellness 18+
- Separate homepage hub inspired by the requested bold pink/purple/black ecommerce layout, with original BLACKED styling and Hinglish copy: “Sanskari outside. Savage inside.”
- New abstract, non-explicit `wellness-hero-art.svg` illustration with care packaging, gift box, leaves and decorative elements; it avoids competitor logos and explicit body/device imagery.
- Four category tiles: For Her, For Him, For Couples and New Ideas.
- Eight concept-category cards with product-care notes; filters, live search and reset control work on the client.
- Expandable educational basics for product materials, cleaning/storage and consent/boundaries.
- 18+ reminder and transparent notices: no real product listings, prices, ratings, inventory, discounts or checkout are configured.

## Existing features retained
- P0 selected-image mapping and separate manual local preview mode.
- P1 product references/gallery and detail views where provided.
- P2 Fit Detective and Fit Passport.
- P3 Style Finder, up-to-three-item comparison, wishlist and style board.
- P4 privacy controls and honest review/policy placeholders.
- Original user-supplied lingerie references, BLACKED concept assets, After Dark collection, platonic Plus-One planner and 18+ private preferences prototype.

## Design and content decisions
- The supplied adult-store screenshots were treated as layout references; the new section does not reuse their third-party logo or unverified promotional numbers/ratings.
- No external store is embedded, scraped or linked. User-supplied branded external images in the existing reference catalogue remain clearly identified as inspiration-only.
- The new wellness category illustrations are design placeholders, not photographic product claims.

## Limitations
- Static frontend prototype only. No account system, age verification, real product inventory, cart, checkout, delivery commitment, customer support service or backend is connected.
- The 18+ reminder is not formal age verification. A real public launch needs age/identity verification, secure accounts, privacy notices/deletion workflows, moderation, block/report tools, platform security and Indian legal/privacy review.
- No product price, stock, rating, discount, shipping claim, material composition or medical claim has been invented.
- Full browser click-through could not be completed in the current environment; see `TEST-NOTES.md`.
